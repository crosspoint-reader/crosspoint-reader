# Xteink X4

ESP32-C3, no PSRAM. 800×480 panel; original controller SSD1677, newer batches
UC8179 / UC8279 (bus probe at boot). Same three drivers as X4 Pro
(`Ssd1677Driver` / `Uc8179Driver` / `Uc8279X4Driver`); do not share one
Half/GC assumption across them. GPIO13 is the battery MOSFET latch
(`power.latch0`; drive LOW to power off). GPIO20 is VBUS detect
(`HalGPIO::isUsbConnected` reads it HIGH). No touch, no frontlight, no home
key.

Shares one C3 binary with X3 via `[env:default]` and the C3 release aliases
(`gh_release`, `gh_release_rc`, `slim`). That env may split later; this file
stays `x4.md`. The compiled framebuffer for that binary is the **max** of X3
and X4 (`MAX_FRAMEBUFFER_BYTES` = 52,272).
