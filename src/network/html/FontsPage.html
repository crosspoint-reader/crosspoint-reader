Convert a font

Makes a font family for the reader from TrueType or OpenType files, here in your browser: the same conversion as the fonts in the catalog.

Choose the family's files, up to four: regular, bold, italic and bold italic. Each file's style is read from the font.

A book on the reader: open it with Details in the File Manager and choose Convert its fonts.
