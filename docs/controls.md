# Controls

Open **Settings → Controls** to configure buttons and touch input. The available
options depend on the device.

- **Home button gestures** opens the existing gesture assignments.
- **Power button** contains **Short click** and, on X4 Pro, **Double click**.
- **Front buttons** contains remapping, orientation, Back behavior, and the
  long-press settings at the bottom.
- **Side buttons** contains the reader layout, orientation, and page-turn long press.
- **Taps and gestures** contains **Touch controls while reading** and the page/menu gestures.

**Page turn long press** is shared by Front buttons and Side buttons. Changing it
in either group changes the same setting.

Choose **Custom** to give each page button its own long-press action. Front buttons then
shows **Front Next long press** and **Front Previous long press**, and Side buttons shows
**Side Next long press** and **Side Previous long press**. Each offers the **Menu long press**
actions plus **Chapter Skip** and **Orientation Change**. The action runs when the button is
released after a hold.

On X4 Pro, **Double click** offers the same actions as **Short click**, plus
**Frontlight** to toggle the light. With a double-click action enabled, a single
click waits up to half a second before its action runs. A double click runs only
its assigned action. Set Double click to **Ignore** to remove this wait.
Reader actions such as page turn and footnotes apply while reading. Sleep,
screen refresh, Confirm, and the frontlight retain their usual behavior.
